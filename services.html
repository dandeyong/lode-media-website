Social Media Management

End-to-end social media management for golf brands, courses and creators. Daily posting, community moderation, DM handling, audience growth and platform strategy across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and X.

Campaign Strategy

Strategic golf campaigns from concept to delivery — product launches, tournament activations, ambassador partnerships and seasonal pushes. Every campaign built around the commercial outcome, then executed by our production team.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about LODE Media, Logan Deyong and how the agency works.

What is LODE Media?

LODE Media is a UK social media agency and content creation agency specialising in golf. Founded in 2026 by golf creator Logan Deyong (@logandeyonggolf), the agency creates content and manages campaigns for golf brands, tournaments, courses and creators.

Who is Logan Deyong?

Logan Deyong is the founder of LODE Media. A UK golf creator with 91,000+ Instagram followers and 100 million+ combined content reach. He was set for a US golf scholarship before the 2020 pandemic redirected his career into social media. He has partnered with LIV Golf, Cobra Puma Golf, BMW UK, Sky Sports and PGA Tour.

Does LODE Media only work with golf brands?

Golf is our specialism, so most of our client work is golf-focused (brands, tournaments, courses, creators). The same content methodology applies to any sport or lifestyle brand where scroll-stopping short-form video drives commercial outcomes.

What services does LODE Media offer?

Content creation (photo, video, Instagram Reels, TikTok), social media management, campaign strategy, growth and engagement, creative direction, and performance reporting. Available full-service or à la carte.

Which brands has LODE Media worked with?

Brand and campaign partners include LIV Golf, Cobra Puma Golf, BMW UK, Sky Sports, PGA Tour, DP World Tour (Genesis Scottish Open), JCB Golf & Country Club, De Vere Wokefield Estate, Stromberg Golf, Holland & Barrett and UPS.

Where is LODE Media based?

LODE Media is a UK-based social media agency. We shoot on location across the UK and travel internationally for tour events, hero shoots and brand campaigns.

How do I hire LODE Media?

Email info@lodemedia.co.uk or use the contact form. We reply within 24 hours.

What platforms does LODE Media create content for?

Instagram (Reels, feed, Stories), TikTok, YouTube (Shorts and long-form), LinkedIn, X (Twitter) and Facebook. Our specialism is short-form vertical video for golf audiences.
